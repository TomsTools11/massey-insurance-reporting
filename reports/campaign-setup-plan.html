03

Targeting, filters & delivery

How both campaigns are configured at launch

Geography

Pennsylvania statewide at launch. We start broad, then concentrate spend on the areas that perform. Home is anchored on the 19317 Brandywine Valley base, with the option to weight priority ZIPs such as West Chester.

Lead filters

Applied on both lines: clean/good driving record, no accidents, no claims, and no SR-22. These focus spend on higher-quality risks that fit the book.

Carrier approach

Geico is excluded from targeting. Progressive is under review pending your confirmation on whether to keep it in or exclude it.

Ad placement

We launch at an efficient placement (around third position) to fit the budget, with room to move up toward second position as performance data supports it.

Lead delivery

Leads are delivered by email, into Ricochet360, and to the GOAL portal. Inbound calls route to your dedicated RingCentral line.

Bid modifiers

None at launch. Source and segment modifiers are introduced once we have enough data to bid up on what converts, reviewed at an upcoming account review.
