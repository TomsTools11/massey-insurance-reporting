07

Recommendations

1
Increase delivery weighting in Southeast Pennsylvania
We are raising the share of daily budget directed at Philadelphia, Montgomery, Delaware, Bucks, Chester and Berks counties.
Metric behind it: 72 of 153 leads (47.1%) and 7 of 15 quotes (46.7%) from six counties.
2
Hold delivery in Western Pennsylvania
Allegheny, Butler, Cambria and Elk counties stay at current weighting while volume accumulates.
Metric behind it: $94.86 Quoted CPA against a $133.59 statewide figure, on 20.3% of leads.
3
Reduce weighting in Central Pennsylvania
We are lowering delivery across Dauphin, Cumberland, York, Lancaster and Lebanon counties and redirecting that budget to the southeast.
Metric behind it: 13.4% of media cost producing 1 of 15 quotes at $268.41 per quote.
4
Record a disposition on every lead
County and city performance is measured on contacted and quoted counts. Leads without a recorded outcome are counted as not contacted, which understates every regional figure.
Metric behind it: 129 of 153 leads have no disposition recorded.
5
Review geography again at 400 leads
33 of the 41 counties with leads have four or fewer. County-level rates are re-cut at the next review once volume supports them.
Metric behind it: 84 of 98 cities at one lead each.
Our current objective

Concentrate delivery in the highest-quoting counties

Each update pairs with the metric that motivated it. Progress is reviewed at the next account review.
