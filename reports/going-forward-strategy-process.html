GOAL Performance Marketing
Massey Insurance · Onboarding 2026

The strategy for this account is to launch broad and make the first 30 days about data collection. Once we have enough data, the focus shifts to using it to drive growth and optimize the campaigns. Performance is reviewed on a recurring biweekly cadence, and each review is driven by the complete quote and bind data you send us beforehand.

01

Our strategy

Start broad, collect data, then optimize and scale

Phase 1 · Days 1–30

Launch broad, collect data

Establish the baseline
  • Launch both campaigns across Pennsylvania with wide targeting
  • Gather data on sources, placements, ZIPs, and lead quality
  • Read performance by leading indicators, which mature faster than cost per policy
  • Hold bids and modifiers steady while the picture forms
Phase 2 · Day 30 onward

Optimize & scale

Turn data into growth
  • Concentrate budget on the sources and segments that convert
  • Calibrate bids and placement; add modifiers to bid up on what works
  • Refine geography and targeting from the data
  • Scale spend on proven performers toward your growth goals

    Flag and adjust anything underdelivering
What you do
  • •
    Respond to every lead within 5 minutes
  • •
    Work each lead across a multi-touch, multi-channel cadence
  • •

Early on, we judge the campaigns by contact rate and quote rate rather than cost per policy. Policies take time to work through the quoting and binding cycle, so leading indicators give the most reliable read in the first weeks.
03

Account review process

A recurring biweekly review, driven by your data

Before every review Vital

Send us your complete quote and bind data ahead of each review. This is the single most important input. Without it, we can only see the top of the funnel — leads and contacts. We cannot see which sources, ZIPs, and segments are actually producing quotes and policies, and those are the decisions that drive optimization. Complete data in means accurate optimization out.

Set a standing time

We schedule a recurring biweekly review so it is on both calendars and never slips.

You send data first

Your full quote and bind data arrives before the call so the review works from complete numbers.

We decide the next moves

Each review sets the specific bid, source, and targeting changes for the next two weeks.
