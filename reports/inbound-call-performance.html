Recommendations

Answer every call on the campaign line
Calls to this line come from prospects who saw the ad and dialed rather than filling out a form. Route the line so it always reaches a licensed producer during business hours, with a named backup.
Metric behind it: 9 of 28 calls (32.1%) went unanswered.
2
Return every unanswered call within 5 minutes
Unanswered calls should generate an immediate callback and a text. The line shows caller ID for every inbound attempt.
Metric behind it: 7 quotes and 1 policy sit in the unanswered calls at the current answered-call conversion rate.
3
Extend coverage to the hours calls arrive
We are pulling the hour-of-day distribution for the next review so staffing can be aligned to call arrival times.
Metric behind it: 67.9% answer rate across 28 calls.

Record call outcomes in the CRM
Quote and policy outcomes logged against each call let us direct budget toward the sources and counties that generate calls that close.
Metric behind it: 2 policies recorded from calls this period.
